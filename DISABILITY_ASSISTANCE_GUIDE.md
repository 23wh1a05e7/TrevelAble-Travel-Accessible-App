# TravelAble - Disability & Assistance Reference Guide

## Disability Types

### 1. Mobility Impairment
**Description:** Physical limitations affecting movement and locomotion

**Symptoms:**
- Difficulty walking
- Uses wheelchair, walker, or crutches
- Limited standing ability
- Balance issues
- Stiff joints or paralysis

**Travel Considerations:**
- Wheelchair accessible transport
- Accessible bathrooms
- Seating near exits
- Accessible transportation terminals
- Help with luggage
- Accessible accommodation

**Example:** Elderly person with arthritis needing walker support

---

### 2. Visual Impairment
**Description:** Partial or complete loss of sight

**Symptoms:**
- Legally blind
- Low vision
- Color blindness
- Night blindness
- Tunnel vision

**Travel Considerations:**
- Audio descriptions
- Braille materials
- Guide dog accommodation
- White cane accessibility
- Verbal announcements
- Digital screen readers

**Example:** Person with diabetic retinopathy needing audio assistance

---

### 3. Hearing Impairment
**Description:** Partial or complete loss of hearing

**Symptoms:**
- Deaf
- Hard of hearing
- Cochlear implant users
- Sign language users
- Hearing aid users

**Travel Considerations:**
- Visual alerts
- Sign language interpreters
- Written communications
- Visual paging systems
- TTY/TTD compatibility
- Captioning services

**Example:** Deaf traveler requiring sign language interpreter

---

### 4. Cognitive Disability
**Description:** Mental or intellectual limitations affecting thinking and learning

**Symptoms:**
- Intellectual disability
- Autism spectrum
- Traumatic brain injury
- Learning disabilities
- Dementia
- Memory loss

**Travel Considerations:**
- Simplified instructions
- Clear signage
- Companion required
- Flexible schedules
- Familiar routes
- Reduced stress environment

**Example:** Person with autism needing quiet space and routine

---

### 5. Chronic Medical Conditions
**Description:** Ongoing health conditions requiring management during travel

**Conditions:**
- Diabetes
- Heart disease
- Respiratory issues
- Kidney disease
- Cancer
- Hypertension
- Arthritis
- Asthma

**Travel Considerations:**
- Medication access
- Medical monitoring
- Oxygen availability
- Refrigeration for medications
- Rest periods
- Medical facilities nearby
- Dietary requirements

**Example:** Diabetic traveler needing insulin management

## Medical Conditions & Recommended Assistance

### Diabetes
```
Medications: Insulin, oral medication
Assistance Types: Medication management, Medical monitoring
Special Needs: Refrigeration, Regular meals, Blood sugar monitoring
Budget: ₹2000-5000/day
Assistant Gender: No preference
```

### Mobility Impairment
```
Medications: Pain relief, muscle relaxants
Assistance Types: Mobility assistance, Personal care, Emergency support
Special Needs: Wheelchair accessibility, Cushioning, Rest periods
Budget: ₹3000-6000/day
Assistant Gender: Female preferred (for personal care)
```

### Hearing Impairment
```
Medications: None typically
Assistance Types: Communication support, Emergency support
Special Needs: Sign language interpreter, Written communication, Visual alerts
Budget: ₹2000-4000/day
Assistant Gender: No preference
```

### Heart Condition
```
Medications: Multiple (Beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors)
Assistance Types: Medical monitoring, Emergency support, Medication management
Special Needs: Stress reduction, Rest periods, Medication storage
Budget: ₹4000-8000/day
Assistant Gender: No preference
```

### Arthritis
```
Medications: Anti-inflammatory, Pain relief
Assistance Types: Mobility assistance, Personal care, Medical monitoring
Special Needs: Warm therapy, Rest periods, Accessible seating
Budget: ₹2500-5000/day
Assistant Gender: Female preferred
```

## FAQ

**Q: Can I travel without medical assistance?**
A: Yes, TravelAble supports all disability types. Assistance is optional based on your needs.

**Q: What if I have multiple conditions?**
A: You can select multiple disability types and assistance types. The form supports this.

**Q: Can I change my request after submitting?**
A: Contact TravelAble support within 24 hours to modify your request.

**Q: How do I get matched with an assistant?**
A: Our team reviews requests and contacts you within 24-48 hours.

**Q: What if I have allergies?**
A: Clearly list all allergies. The assistant will be informed before travel.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds available if service is cancelled 48 hours before travel.
